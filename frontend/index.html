DECISION STUDIO // FRAME THE QUESTION

TURN AN AMBIGUOUS INTENTION INTO A TESTABLE DECISION

Define what is changing, what success means, what cannot be violated, and which data should be used. This prevents Scenario Lab from producing attractive but meaningless futures.

Boardroom quick starts:
Delay regional launch by 2 weeks Add weekend support coverage for 4 weeks Redirect 20% freight volume to Northeast Introduce 60-day renewal service credit
1 Change: What will be different from the baseline?
Why we ask this
State the operational or strategic change in precise, measurable terms.
2 Objective: The outcome you want to improve
Why we ask this
The primary executive metric or goal the decision is intended to optimize.
3 Constraints: Non-negotiable boundaries
Why we ask this
Boundaries that cannot be violated, such as budget, safety, capacity, or contractual commitments.
4 Time Horizon: When effects should be measured
Why we ask this
The observation window over which primary and second-order effects will unfold.
